Side lengths are shown in grid units, so you can compare them at a glance. A triangle's side-type (scalene / isosceles / equilateral) and its angle-type (acute / right / obtuse) are two separate ways of describing the same shape — try the presets below and see which combinations show up together.

Equilateral

All 3 sides equal. All 3 angles equal 60°.

Isosceles

Exactly 2 sides equal (and the angles opposite them, too).

Scalene

No sides equal. No angles equal.

Right

One angle is exactly 90°.

Acute

Every angle is less than 90°.

Obtuse

One angle is greater than 90°.
