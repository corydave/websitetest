CSC 150 A demonstration

Choosing a model, blind

Three tasks, two answers each, and no labels until you have voted. It takes about four minutes, and the interesting part is not which one wins.

Everything on this page was written by hand. These are not real model responses. Nothing here was captured from any AI system, and no response is attributed to any real product - the reveal tells you what each one was written to demonstrate instead.

The format, where you commit to a vote before you see anything identifying, is the one public model arenas use. This is a teaching model of that idea rather than one of them; arena.ai is the real thing.

Why blind

You are rating the writing, not the badge

Told which system produced which answer, most people rate the one they already trust more highly - the same answer, scored differently. Covering the labels is the cheapest way to find out what you actually think, and the gap between your blind votes and your stated favourite is the whole exercise.
