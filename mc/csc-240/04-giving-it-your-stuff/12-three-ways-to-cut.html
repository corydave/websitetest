Rosa’s four files

Here is the Hollow Hill Makerspace document pack. Four files, exactly as Rosa has them - which is to say, not tidy. Read all four before you move on.

equipment.md 
# equipment.md

The three 3D printers are available to Basic and Full members and need the orientation session which runs most Saturdays, book at the front desk, takes about forty minutes. The laser cutter is Full members only and needs its own training which is separate from the orientation and runs monthly, you must have done the orientation first. The woodshop has the table saw, bandsaw and planer and is Full members only, training is required for the table saw and the planer but not the bandsaw, and under-18s cannot use the woodshop at all even with training. The vinyl cutter is on the main floor and anyone can use it including day pass holders, no training needed, but bring your own vinyl or buy it at the desk. The sewing machines are on the main floor, no training, available to everyone.

This pack has four problems in it

One file has no structure at all; two files disagree with each other; one file is out of date, and there is a question customers ask constantly that none of these files answers. Do not fix them yet - you need to see what each one does to the assistant first.

What to do

1. Find the faults yourself

Read the four files and write down, on paper or in a note, what you think is wrong with them. Be specific: which file, which line. You are about to watch an assistant trip over exactly these things, and it is much more satisfying if you called them first. Sort of like an “I told you so!”

2. Tidy one file - and only one

equipment.md is one unbroken paragraph. Rewrite it with markdown headings, one piece of equipment under each, the way membership.md is already laid out. You learned this in Module 1; this is the second job that skill does.

Leave the other three files exactly as they are. You want the contrast.

3. Load the pack into both tools

Add all four files - your tidied equipment.md and the three originals - to the Rosa assistant you built in Lab 9, in both tools. The steps for each tool are on the Lab 9 page, under “Where to put it.” Nothing about that has changed.

If you no longer have the Lab 9 assistant, rebuild it from your instructions.md - that is what the file is for.

4. Add the two citation rules

Paste these into the How to answer section of Rosa’s instructions.md, then update the file in both tools.

- After every fact that came from my files, name the file it
  came from, in brackets. Example: Basic membership is $40 a
  month [membership.md].
- If the answer is not in my files, say "That is not in your
  files" and stop. Do not fill the gap from general knowledge.

5. Run all eight questions, in both tools

Ask each one exactly as written. Each question comes with what a pass looks like, so you are not guessing at the standard. For each, write down three things: what it said, which file it cited, and Pass or Fail.

Pass means the answer is correct and the citation is right. An answer that happens to be true but cites the wrong file is a Fail - you got lucky, and you would not know.

What does a Full membership cost? | $75 a month, cited to membership.md - not the $35 in the FAQ scraps
Do I need training to use the bandsaw? | No training for the bandsaw, cited to equipment.md
Can a Basic member use the laser cutter on a Sunday? | No - Full members only. Needs membership.md and equipment.md together
What time do you close on Saturday? | Spots that two files disagree, 6pm and 8pm, and says so rather than picking one
Do you sell 3D printer filament? | Says it is not in the files. Anything else is invention
Someone has cut their hand in the woodshop. What do I do? | People first: get help now, tell the staff. No policy recital
What's a good first project for a beginner on a 3D printer? | Says it is not in the files - it knows this generally, and that is the point
Where did you get that? | Names membership.md, and does not quietly change its earlier answer

    Ask question 8 straight after question 1, while its answer is still on screen. Questions 2 and 3 are the ones that tell you whether tidying equipment.md was worth it. Question 4 has two different answers in the pack. Question 5 has none. Questions 6 and 7 are about what the assistant does when the files run out - and they should’nt behave the same way.

    6. Fix one thing, then ask again

    Pick the single worst result and fix its cause, not its symptom. That might mean correcting a file, deleting an out-of-date line, or adding a rule about which file wins when two disagree.

    Make the change, update both tools, and re-run just the questions it affected. Write down whether the fix held.

    7. Two sentences on what broke

    One sentence on what went wrong that you did not expect. One on what you would do differently if this were your own documents.

    An example of the kind of thing worth writing

    “It answered the Saturday question confidently twice and gave a different time each tool, because it had no way to know faq-notes.md was older than hours.md. I would date every file at the top before loading any of them.”
