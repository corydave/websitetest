Part 1 · Write it

From your map to its instructions

Every AI step in your map needs a rule here. Every check you wrote, and every handoff, becomes part of how the assistant answers. Bring your map in, so its AI steps sit beside you as you write.

Made your map in this browser? It may already be here - press Bring in my map. Otherwise open workflow-map.md and paste the whole file into the box. Your map is only read, never changed.

Paste workflow-map.md instead

Write the four sections

Same four sections as Maria’s file and Rosa’s. Fill in each box; the page builds instructions.md from them as you type.

Who it helps, with which job - the task from your map. Then what it is not for, so it says so instead of guessing.

One rule per AI step: what to do, and what to hand back - a table, a list, a draft. That is the handoff from last week. Your checks belong here too: “say which line of the notes each action item came from” makes your check possible.

What could hurt, embarrass or mislead someone if it went wrong? Start there. Anything that should stay a “Me” step - sending, deciding, contacting people - is a Never for the assistant.

What should it do when the input is messy, something is missing, or the request is not its job? “Ask me” beats a confident guess.

       Part 3 · Test it
      
    Test it on real work
    

    Three tests, each in a fresh chat inside your project. Use real work, or made-up stand-ins if the real thing is private - the same rule as Week 1.
    
    
    

    
    
    
      Test 1
      
    A real run. Give it what it would get on a normal week - this week’s notes, sheet or questions. Does what comes back pass the checks in your map?
    
    
    
    
    
      Test 2
      
    A messy run. Something missing, out of order, or ambiguous. Does it follow your “When you are unsure” section, or guess?
    
    
    
    
    
      Test 3
      
    Something it must refuse. Ask it to do something on your Never list. Push a little, the way a real person would. Does it hold?
    
    
    

    
    
      
    Fix one thing: version 2
    

    Pick what the tests showed you. Change the rule in the boxes above, paste the new file into your tool, and run that test again. Say what changed, so next month’s you knows why.

       The rubric
      
    How it is graded
    

    Five criteria, up to four points each, twenty points in all.
    
    
    
    
    CriterionNot yet - 0Meets - 2Strong - 4
    Scoped to the jobA general helper, or no “What you are for”Says what it is for, matching the task in your mapAlso says what it is not for, so it can turn other jobs away
    A rule for every AI stepAI steps from your map with no ruleEvery AI step in your map has a rule saying what to doEach rule also says what to hand back - the format the next step needs
    What it must refuseNo Never listAt least two Nevers that matter for this task, and what to do when unsureAt least one Never protects a person - their privacy, a message they receive, or a decision that is theirs
    Tested for realNo tests, or only “hello” chatsThree tests logged - real, messy, must refuse - with what happenedOne change made because of a test, and the retest
    Saved and kept currentOnly on this pageSaved in a real tool, with the tool and the assistant’s nameThe file says which version it is and what changed
    
    
    The AI-use statement is not a criterion
    Work without one comes back unmarked so you can add it - not marked down, just not marked yet. Same rule as every Build.
