So every card gets a caption - words under the picture - and every picture gets a real description in its alt.

But you counted five file names scattered through five separate blocks in the last lab, and adding a sixth picture meant copying a whole block and editing it carefully. Adding captions and descriptions the same way would mean fifteen scattered things instead of five.

So they are not going to be scattered. All the words about all the pictures are going to sit in one list at the very top of the script, where you can find them, and the code underneath will read from that list.

That split - the content up top where a human can reach it, the code below getting on with its job - turns out to be one of the most useful shapes in software. You are going to keep meeting it.

Find the list. What three things does each picture have in it?

A file name, a caption, and a description - something like { file: "photo-1.jpg", caption: "Sunrise over the lake", alt: "An orange sky above a flat grey lake" }. The names on the left may differ; the three things will be there. Each set of curly braces is one picture. The whole list sits inside square brackets.

What separates one picture’s entry from the next?

A comma. One at the end of every entry except, usually, the last. That small comma is about to be the most important punctuation mark in your week - see the Grow step, and the first Troubleshooting card.

If you changed a caption in that list, how many other places would you have to change to match?

None. That is the entire reason for doing it this way. One place to look, one place to change, and no way to leave the page half-updated.

One - add a sixth picture, without asking the AI anything.

This is the payoff, and you are going to do it by hand in about ninety seconds:

  1. Put another picture in the pictures folder and name it photo-6.jpg.
  2. In the list at the top, find the second-to-last entry. Put your cursor at the end of it, after the closing brace and its comma.
  3. Add a new entry on its own line, in the same shape as its neighbours, with your new file name, a caption and a description.
  4. Save. Refresh. Six pictures.

Add it in the middle, not at the end. The last entry in a list usually has no comma after it, so adding after it is where people get caught. Adding between two existing entries means you are copying a line that already has its comma in the right place, and the trap never fires. Same trick works in every list you will ever edit.

Stop and notice what just happened. You changed what the app shows without reading a single line of the code that shows it. Last lab that took a careful copy-and-paste of a whole block; this lab it took one line. That is what the list bought you.

I added an entry and the whole page went blank

This is the one. It is almost always a missing comma, and it will happen to nearly everybody once.

Open the Developer Console. You will see a red line saying something like SyntaxError: Unexpected token with a line number after the file name. Go to that line in your editor and look at the line above it - a list entry that should end with a comma and doesn’t.

Why blank, rather than just a missing picture? Because the browser reads the whole list before it runs anything, and a list it cannot make sense of stops it dead. One comma, one blank page. It feels wildly out of proportion, and it is, and every programmer alive has lost an evening to it.

Add the comma. Save. Refresh.

My caption has an apostrophe in it and now the page is blank

Grandma’s kitchen inside single quotes ends the text early at the apostrophe, and everything after it becomes nonsense. Two fixes: wrap the caption in double quotes instead - "Grandma's kitchen" - or leave a backslash before the apostrophe. Double quotes are easier to remember. Same console error as the comma, same way of finding it.

I added an entry and now one card is empty

The list is fine - the file isn’t there, or isn’t named what the entry says. Check the folder for photo-6.jpg, spelled exactly as you typed it in the list. This is the good failure: the app read your new entry perfectly and told you the picture is missing.

My descriptions don’t show up anywhere

Correct. alt is not meant to be seen when the picture is fine. To prove yours is working, misspell one file name in the list, save and refresh - your description appears where the picture was. Put it back afterwards.

The order of the pictures changed

The slider shows them in list order now, not in the order they were written into the page before. Move the entries around in the list until you like it - that is the list doing its job, and rearranging your slider is now a matter of cutting and pasting lines.
