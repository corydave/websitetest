Before the build, two words. They are worth more to you than most of the code in this course.

A card is a box that holds one thing. A picture, a heading, a couple of lines, maybe a button. Rounded corners, a bit of shadow, a bit of space around the edge. You have looked at ten thousand of them without ever needing the word.

A slider shows one card at a time, with a way to move to the next one. Some people call it a carousel. Same thing.

Here is why that matters. Ask an AI to “make the pictures go sideways” and it will guess, and you will get something. Ask it for “a slider of cards” and it knows exactly what you mean, because those are the words the people who build these things use. Learning the name of a thing is most of the work of asking for it.

Today: five of your pictures, five cards, one slider.

Start a new chat. Describe it in your own words, and make sure it says:

  • A slider of five cards, showing one card at a time
  • Each card holds one picture
  • Next and Previous buttons to move between them
  • It should wrap around, so Next on the last card goes back to the first
  • The pictures will be files called photo-1.jpg through photo-5.jpg, in the same folder as the page
  • All the pictures should appear at the same size, however big the files are

That last line saves you a lot of grief. Five photos off a phone will be five different shapes, and without it your slider will jump about as it moves between them.

That is five renames, which is five chances to typo. If one card comes up blank, you have not failed the lab - you have found the one file whose name doesn’t match, and the Troubleshooting tells you which. This is the lab where the naming habit stops being advice and starts being muscle memory.

Open index.html in your editor and look at the <body>.

Find the card holding photo-3.jpg. What is wrapped around that <img>?

A <div>, almost certainly with class="card" or something close. A <div> is a plain box with no meaning of its own - it exists so the styling has something to grab. The word card in that line is the same word you used in your prompt, which is a nice thing to notice: you asked for a card and got a thing literally called a card.

Count how many times a file name appears in this file.

Five - one per card, each in its own src, scattered down the page in five separate blocks. That works, and it is about to become annoying. Hold that thought.

What would you have to do to add a sixth picture?

Put photo-6.jpg in the folder, then find a card block, copy the whole thing, paste it in the right place, and change the file name inside it - being careful not to break anything on the way. Doable. Tedious. Easy to get wrong. Remember this answer; the next lab makes it a great deal shorter.

Swap one of the pictures, without touching the code.

Pick a different picture from your phone or your computer. Put it in the pictures folder. Rename the current photo-2.jpg to something else - photo-2-old.jpg will do - and then rename your new picture to photo-2.jpg.

Save nothing, edit nothing. Just refresh the page.

Card two is now a different picture. The code never mentioned any particular photograph; it only ever asked for a name. Whatever is sitting at that name is what shows up. That is a bigger idea than it looks, and the whole next lab leans on it.

One card is blank, or shows a broken picture

One file name out of five does not match. Right-click the blank spot, choose Inspect, and read the src on the highlighted line - that is the name the page wants. Now look in the folder for the file that isn’t named that. It is usually a capital, a missing hyphen (photo1.jpg instead of photo-1.jpg), or .jpeg where the code says .jpg.

All five pictures show at once, stacked down the page

You got five cards but not a slider. Tell the AI: “show only one card at a time, with Next and Previous moving between them.” If the buttons are there but everything is still visible, the part that hides the other cards is missing or broken - open the Developer Console and see if there is a red line, and paste it back if there is.

The buttons do nothing

Open the Developer Console before anything else. A red line when you click means the button’s code broke, and the message is the fix - copy it exactly and paste it back to the AI. No red line at all usually means the buttons were never wired up: “the Next and Previous buttons don’t do anything when I click them.”

The page jumps around as I move between pictures

Your photos are different shapes, so each card is a different height. Ask for: “give every card the same fixed size, and crop the pictures to fit rather than squashing them.” The word you want is crop - squashing makes everybody look strange.

Next on the last picture does nothing

The wrap-around got left out. “When I press Next on the last picture it should go back to the first, and Previous on the first should go to the last.” Going off the end of a list is one of the classic places code breaks, and you just found one in the wild.

Some of my photos are .HEIC

Same as the recipe card: browsers mostly cannot read that format, and renaming will not help. Convert each one to JPG first - Preview on a Mac, Photos on Windows - and name the result photo-2.jpg or whichever it is.

The page takes a long time to appear

Five phone photos is easily fifty megabytes, and the browser loads them all. It still works; it just sighs first. Export them smaller if it bothers you. It is a good thing to have felt, because everyone who visits a slow page feels exactly this and most of them leave.
