Pick something you actually make. A real recipe, a sandwich you are unreasonably proud of, a drink, a thing you feed the dog. Today it becomes a card: a title, a photo, the ingredients, the steps.

Every app you have built so far has been one file that needed nothing else. You just proved it in the tidy-up - you moved all of them and nothing broke. This one is different. This one needs a photo, and a photo is a second file, and the page has to be able to find it.

That is the whole chapter in one build. Everything after this is practice.

Start a new chat and describe the card in your own words. Make sure it says:

  • One recipe, on a single card in the middle of the page
  • A title at the top, then a photo, then the ingredients as a bulleted list, then the steps as a numbered list
  • The photo will be a file called photo.jpg, sitting in the same folder as the page
  • The photo should not be enormous - a few hundred pixels wide is plenty

That third line is the new piece of prompt craft, and it is worth saying out loud: you decided the file name and told the AI to use it. Left to itself the AI will invent a name, or reach out to the internet for a stock picture of soup. Naming the file first means the code and the folder agree before either one exists.

Then paste the specification at the end of your prompt, exactly as it is.

{{spec}}
  1. Make a new folder: vibe-coding/recipe.
  2. Copy the code, paste it into TXTEDTR, and Save as index.html inside that folder.
  3. Now get your photo. Take one with your phone and send it to yourself, or use any picture you have. Put it in the recipe folder, beside index.html.
  4. Rename it to photo.jpg - all lowercase, and typed exactly. This is the step that decides whether the next one works.
  5. Double-click index.html.

Got a broken picture icon instead of your photo? Good. Genuinely - you have just met the most common problem in this entire chapter, in the safest possible place, and the first Troubleshooting card below fixes it in about a minute. Nearly everybody hits this once.

Open index.html in your editor and find three things.

Find the <img> tag. What is written inside src="…"?

photo.jpg, if the AI did what you asked. That is the entire instruction: look in this folder for a file with this name. No path, no folder, no address - just a name, because the file is right there.

Find alt="…" on the same tag. What did the AI write in it?

Probably something vague, like Recipe photo or the name of the dish. It could not see your picture, so it guessed. Those words are what a blind visitor hears instead of the photo, so a guess is not good enough. You are about to fix it.

Find the ingredients. What tag is each single ingredient wrapped in?

<li>, one per ingredient, all sitting inside a <ul>. The steps are the same shape inside an <ol>. To add an ingredient you copy one <li> line and change the words, which is exactly what you will do in a moment.

Write a real alt. Replace whatever the AI guessed with a description of your actual photo, as if you were describing it to someone over the phone. A cast iron pan of cornbread, still in the pan, with one slice cut out. Save, refresh. Nothing on the page changes, which is the point - it is there for people who cannot see it.

Then prove it works. Change src="photo.jpg" to src="photoo.jpg", with the extra letter. Save, refresh. Your photo vanishes and your own words appear in its place. That is alt doing its second job, and it is also exactly what a misspelled file name looks like.

Put the spelling back. Save, refresh. Photo returns.

You just caused the broken picture on purpose, which means it will never scare you again.

I get a broken picture icon, or my alt words instead of the photo

The page looked in its folder for that exact name and did not find it. Check three things in this order:

  1. Is the photo in the recipe folder? Not in Downloads, not in Photos, not on the Desktop.
  2. Does the name match exactly? Right-click the broken picture, choose Inspect, and read the src in the highlighted line. Compare it to the real file character by character. Photo.jpg, photo.JPG, photo.jpeg and photo copy.jpg are four different names.
  3. Is the extension actually showing? If you cannot see .jpg on the end of the file, turn extensions back on (setup lab) - you may have named it photo.jpg.jpg without knowing.

It is almost never the code.

My photo is a .HEIC file and nothing shows

iPhones save photos in a format browsers mostly cannot read. Renaming it to .jpg will not work - the inside of the file has to change, not just the name. On a Mac, open it in Preview → File → Export → choose JPEG. On Windows, open it in Photos → Save as → JPG. Then name the result photo.jpg. To stop it happening again: iPhone Settings → Camera → Formats → Most Compatible.

The AI used a picture from the internet instead of mine

Common, and it looks like success until you notice the photo isn’t yours. Look at the src - if it starts with http, it is pointing at somebody else’s server. Tell the AI: “use a local file called photo.jpg in the same folder, not a picture from the internet.” A page that depends on a stranger’s website breaks the day they delete it.

My photo is sideways or upside down

Phone cameras store the picture one way and tuck a note inside the file saying which way up it should be, and not everything reads that note. Easiest fix: open the photo on your computer, rotate it, save it, and let the file itself be the right way round. There is a whole hidden world of notes tucked inside photo files, and it gets a lab of its own later in the course.

The photo is gigantic, or the page takes a moment to appear

A photo straight off a phone can be several thousand pixels wide and many megabytes. The browser shrinks it to fit but still has to load the whole thing. Ask the AI: “limit the photo to 400 pixels wide.” That fixes how it looks. To fix the size of the file itself, open it in Preview or Photos and export it smaller.

I don’t have a photo of it

Use any picture at all - your shoes, the sky, the dog. The lesson is the file name, not the subject. You can swap in a real photo the next time you cook it, and by then changing it will take you ten seconds.
